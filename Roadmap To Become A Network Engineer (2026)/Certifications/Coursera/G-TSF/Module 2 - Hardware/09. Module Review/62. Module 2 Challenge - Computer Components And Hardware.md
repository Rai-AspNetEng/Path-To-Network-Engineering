<h1>Module 2 challenge: Computer Components and Hardware</h1>

1. Which computer component is a connection point that allows a computer to connect to other devices? - <b>Port</b>
2. The CPU is able to get data most quickly from which of the following storage locations? - <b>Cache</b>
3. Which of the following are types of RAM? Select all that apply. - <b>DDR SDRAM and DRAM</b>
4. Tablets and e-readers are examples of: - <b>Mobile Devices</b> 
5. Which of the following can cause damage to rechargeable batteries? Select all that apply. - <b>very cold and hot environments, also by using a wrong charger</b>
6. What units are commonly used to represent network data transfer rates? - <b>Mb/s</b>
7. A malfunctioning motherboard component is causing issues with your computer's graphics/video and memory. Which chip on the motherboard is most likely the culprit? - <b>Northbridge</b>
8. You're looking to buy a new hard drive and want the fastest option for data access. Which of these RPM speeds likely indicates the fastest hard drive? - <b>10,000 rpm</b>
9. Why would a user need a bigger power supply for your computer? Select all that apply. - <b>The user is a videographer and edits a lot of video content, and also plays a lot of high resolution games.</b>
10. You're presenting at a conference and hook up your laptop to the projector. When you turn it on, the image is blurry and pixelated, nowhere near the quality you were expecting. What's the most likely culprit for this issue? - <b>Your computer did not recognize the display resolution of the projector and needs a device driver.</b>
