<h1>Glossary Terms From Course 1 (Module 1)</h1>

<b>New terms and their definitions: Course 1 Module 1</b>

1. <b>Abstraction:</b> To take a relatively complex system and simplify it for our use

2. <b>Algorithm:</b> A series of steps that solves specific problems

3. <b>ASCII:</b> The oldest character encoding standard used is ASCII. It represents the English alphabet, digits, and punctuation marks

4. <b>Binary system:</b> The communication that a computer uses is referred to as binary system, also known as base-2 numeral system

5. <b>Byte:</b> A group of 8 bits

6. <b>Character encoding:</b> Is used to assign our binary values to characters so that we as humans can read them

7. <b>Computer:</b> A device that stores and processes data by performing calculations

8. <b>Cryptography:</b> The overarching discipline that covers the practice of coding and hiding messages from third parties

9. <b>Decimal form- base 10 system:</b> In the decimal system, there are 10 possible numbers you can use ranging from zero to nine

10. <b>Digital divide:</b> The growing skills gap between people with and without digital literacy skills

11. <b>Information technology:</b> The use of digital technology, like computers and the internet, to store and process data into useful information

12. <b>Linux OS:</b> Linux is one of the largest open source operating systems used heavily in business infrastructure and in the consumer space

13. <b>Logic gates:</b> Allow transistors to do more complex tasks, like decide where to send electrical signals depending on logical conditions

14. <b>Open source:</b> This means the developers will let other developers share, modify, and distribute their software for free

15. <b>PDA (Personal Digital Assistant):</b> Allows computing to go mobile

16. <b>Punch cards:</b> A sequence of cards with holes in them to automatically perform calculations instead of manually entering them by hand

17. <b>RGB model:</b> RGB or red, green, and blue model is the basic model of representing colors

18. <b>UTF-8:</b> The most prevalent encoding standard used today
